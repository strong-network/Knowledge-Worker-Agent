# Meeting notes

Turn the user's transcript or notes into meeting notes.

## Steps

1. Read the whole input before writing anything.
2. Write the notes in this order:
   - **Summary**: two or three sentences on what the meeting was for and what
     came out of it.
   - **Decisions**: one line each.
   - **Actions**: one line each, with the owner and the due date when the input
     gives them. Write "no owner" or "no date" rather than guessing.
   - **Open questions**: what was raised but not settled.
3. Leave out small talk and repetition.
4. If the input names no decisions or actions, say so instead of inventing them.

A skill can also hold scripts and templates next to this file. This one needs
none.
